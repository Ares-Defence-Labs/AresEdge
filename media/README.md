# AresScan demonstration media

`scanning-demo.gif` and `scanning-demo.mp4` show live document tracking and the
capture countdown in AresScan+. Both are 4.25-second extracts from Ares Defence
Labs' existing app recording. The GIF loops at 12 fps and 320 pixels wide; the
MP4 is silent H.264 at 24 fps and 540 pixels wide.

`scanner.webp` shows the scanning screen. Additional AresScan app screenshots
(`image-filters.webp` and `image-adjustments.webp`) are retained as app media;
those editing tools are not included in the first AresEdge SDK release.

Full videos: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=au0fnn8yGHE (demo) and
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QoQ2w-9bvho (trailer).
